## Macro thesis

Three forces define the late-August 2026 tape, and this book is built to be long all three
and short their mirror image:

1. **AI capital expenditure is still accelerating.** Memory, storage and AI servers are the
   earnings engine of the index. Micron is up ~220% YTD; Dell has an AI server backlog of
   $51.3B and raised FY27 guidance to $165–169B.
2. **Oil is rising on Persian Gulf conflict risk.** Energy has printed all-time highs. The
   same crude strength is a direct cost line for airlines and a driver of sticky inflation.
3. **Bond yields are elevated and rising.** The 10-year has climbed all year and the Treasury's
   plan to subdue yields failed on 19 August (Dow -700). That compresses housing affordability
   and long-duration multiples.

The shorts are the losers of that exact regime: AI-impaired white-collar labour economics,
fuel-squeezed transport, and rate-squeezed housing.

**Deliberately avoided:** Salesforce and the SaaS complex. CRM rallied 22% on 27–28 August on
a record earnings beat and the Anthropic "Claudeforce" partnership. The "AI disrupts SaaS"
short is being actively unwound — that is the wrong side of a live squeeze.

## Risks

- **Factor concentration.** Long FANG and short UAL are the same crude bet expressed twice.
  If oil reverses hard, both legs lose together. This book is dollar-neutral, not factor-neutral.
- **AI correlation.** MU, DELL and VST are ~30% of gross on one theme. Chip stocks already
  sold off on 23 August; a single memory-pricing datapoint moves all three.
- **Crowded shorts.** ACN, LEN and NKE are each already 24–47% off their highs. Beaten-down
  names squeeze violently on any good news — the CRM move is the live example.
- **Dividend carry.** Short NKE (~4%) and short PAYX (~3.6–4.2%) cost roughly $800/yr in
  dividends payable. Longs FANG and GS partially offset.
- **Seasonality.** Strategists flag mid-September onward as the caution window.
- **Borrow.** All five shorts are large-cap and generally easy to borrow, but confirm rates
  and availability with the broker before sizing.
