## The problem with this book

**The regime trade is now consensus.** At 58% priced, shorting rate-sensitives was an edge; at
90%, you are paid to be right about something everyone already knows. And chasing energy longs
after a $93 → $103 move in eight sessions is late.

So Book III does not repeat the trade. It buys the parts of the shock that have *not* moved —
oilfield services, natural gas, nitrogen, insurance float, ethane crackers — and shorts the
rate-sensitive durables complex, which is where deterioration is actually verifiable.

- **Four of five longs are the energy complex.** More concentrated than Book II, not less. The

  re-rates all three together.

  This is the cheapest of the three books to hold.
