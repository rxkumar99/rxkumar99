What is actually going on

What did you buy?
Shares of GDX. One share is a tiny slice of about fifty gold-mining companies at once — Newmont, Barrick, Agnico Eagle and others. You own just over two slices.
Why does the number move?
Other people buy and sell GDX all day long, and the price moves with them. It only moves Monday to Friday while the stock market is open. On weekends and holidays it sits perfectly still.
Did you really lose money on a red day?
Not yet. The loss is only on paper until you sell. If you hold on and the price comes back up, the red day never cost you anything.
Why is Mitra's number so much bigger?
Because Mitra put in more money — $246 against Dhruv's $38. Both of you gain and lose the same percentage; the dollar amounts just scale with what you put in.
